Michael T. Joyce — Coach Video Masterclass¶

Coach Profile & Overview

Michael T. Joyce

Recognized as one of the most adept transitional coaches in WTA history, Michael Joyce has notably propelled Maria Sharapova and Jessie Pegula from their junior beginnings to the pinnacle of tennis. His wealth of coaching insights, techniques, and strategies is extensively shared on CoachLife. With a 20-year tenure as a touring coach on the WTA circuit, Joyce has collaborated with tennis luminaries such as Victoria Azarenka, Sofia Kenin, and Genie Bouchard. Transitioning from a successful professional career, where he achieved a top 60 global ranking and victories over top 10 players, Joyce began working with Maria Sharapova at 14, playing a crucial role in her ascent, in collaboration with fellow CoachLife contributor, Robert Lansdorp. Michael’s CoachLife offerings are replete with innovative content, showcasing techniques like the use of adjustable targets at the net—a legendary and unique target practice drill adopted by some of the greatest players in history. Additionally, his insights into the reverse forehand, a technique employed by icons including Sharapova, Rafael Nadal, and Pete Sampras, further underscore his contribution to the sport. Joyce’s methodological approach, blending strategic gameplay with targeted technical improvements, exemplifies cutting-edge coaching, making his contributions invaluable for players aiming to transcend their current levels. Michael also started working with current WTA number 68 Ashlyn Kruger at only 16 years old and has played a pivotal role in her ascent to number 68, with her ranking rapidly rising as one of the most exciting WTA projects. His content includes vital insights on tennis parenting, the Pete Sampras development method, and a must-see first and second serve return masterclass. More content from Michael will be available soon, including a comprehensive drill section set to be added in July, promising even more valuable resources for advancing tennis skills.
