Foundation Deep Dives¶

Long-form articles on specific foundation topics. Use these when you want to go deeper on a particular aspect of your practice.

The Master Reference¶

  • The Complete Manual — the entire Tennis Knowledge Base, in one place. The longest article. Read it when you have an hour, or use it as a reference when a question comes up.

The 1-Page Pocket Card¶

  • 1-Page Match Pocket Card — The Complete Grip System — printable one-page reference. Tape it to your bag.

The Strokes¶

Detailed biomechanics and practice systems for every stroke:

  • Backhand — the two-hander, one-hander, slice family
  • Forehand — modern topspin, unit turn, contact out front
  • Serve — the whole motion, broken down
  • Volley — body control, no swing
  • Overhead — the smash
  • Doubles Patterns — pattern play, poaching, communication
  • Doubles Serves — where to serve when
  • Lob and Overhead — the high-percentage choices
  • Slice Family Doubles — the slice as a doubles weapon
  • Footwork — split step, recovery, unit turn

The Grips¶

  • Foundations and Grip — the four grips, when to use each
  • Continental Grip — the volley/serve/slice grip
  • Eastern Semi-Western Grip — the all-court grip
  • Grip Change Map — the transitions between grips
  • Grip Pressure — the 3-to-4 pressure system

New Additions (2026-07)¶

  • Footwork Deep Dive: Court Positioning & Recovery Patterns — backpedal vs. shuffle vs. crossover recovery, and the "interceptive strike zone" positioning concept

How to Use This Section¶

These articles are reference material. When a specific question comes up — why does my backhand break down on high balls? How do I time the serve? What's the right doubles formation here? — find the article, read it, take it to the court.

The articles are also sequenced. The Complete Manual is the master. The 1-Page Pocket Card is the summary. The individual stroke articles are the deep-dives. Read in that order if you have the time; pick what you need if you don't.
