# The Serve - Probation

**The Serve: Probation**

**Dr. Brian Gordon**

In the final segment of Brian\'s original series on teaching the serve,
he explains his fundamental rule for synchronizing the backswing and the
leg drive: the concept of probation. Brian sees a universal problem in
junior tennis. The tendency of the racket to enter the backswing far too
early which destroys the impact of the leg drive and its ability to
drive the racket down the back. See how he teaches the probation
position and his surprising take on what creates racket speed - and what
diminishes it.
