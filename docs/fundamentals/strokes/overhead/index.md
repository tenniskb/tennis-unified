# Overhead Fundamentals

The overhead is a serve you don't control the toss for. Same kinetic chain as the serve — track, move, brake, snap. See [Serve Fundamentals](../serve/index.md) for the complete kinetic chain breakdown.

## Key Overhead Differences from Serve
- **No controlled toss** — you must track and move to the ball
- **Side-on stance** — turn immediately, non-dominant hand points at ball
- **Crossover steps** — never backpedal (causes falling backward)
- **Contact point** — slightly in front, to the right (right-hander), above head
- **Racket drop** — let gravity + trunk rotation drop racket behind back; don't muscle it down
- **Brake & Snap** — front hip/leg brakes hard, torso stops, arm whips, pronates

## Fault Signals

| Symptom | Root Cause | Fix |
|---------|------------|-----|
| Goes long | Contact behind head, or face open | Move feet, contact in front, pronate |
| Into net | Racket dropped late, or muscled down | Let gravity drop racket, let trunk rotate |
| Falling backward | Backpedaled, no root | Crossover steps, wide base, front foot brakes |

## Key Points
1. Turn sideways immediately — non-dominant hand points at ball
2. Move with crossover steps, NEVER backpedal
3. Contact: slightly in front, to right, above head
4. Let racket drop by gravity + trunk rotation; don't muscle it down
5. Front hip/leg brakes, torso stops, arm whips, pronates
6. Eyes stay on contact spot until ball leaves strings; no peeking
7. Finish: racket crosses body, weight forward, balanced

**Coach's Tip:** Overhead fear = looking at target too soon. Drill: hit overheads, call "contact" instant you feel ball on strings. Only then look. 20 balls — peek early = partner calls it.
