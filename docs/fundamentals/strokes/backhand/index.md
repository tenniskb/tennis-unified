# Backhand Fundamentals

Complete technical guide to the one-handed saber and two-handed hybrid backhand systems.

## The Saber System Map (1H Backhand)

| Variable | Name | Saber Implementation |
|----------|------|----------------------|
| Q | Quiet Eye | Eyes park higher and further to the side; chin over front shoulder, staying side-on |
| V | Vestibular | Head stays side-on, chin over front shoulder — never turning to face the net |
| ROOT | Rooting | Front foot root holds side-on position; back leg pushes, shear snaps the lat |
| 8 | Figure-8 | Vertical/diagonal 8: high entry behind, crossover drop, up-and-forward exit |
| 45° | Plane | Diagonal glass — high behind, low in front; cut down to go up |
| Brake | Impulse | Chest brakes side-on; lat and tricep snap edge through — 1-inch saber pulse |
| Pulse | Fa jin | Edge slices down through ball; face squares at last instant |
| Jin | Kình | Ting feels lat stretch; Hua absorbs high ball pace; Fa is saber snap |

## Load Phase: As the Ball Leaves the Opponent
- **Eyes:** Soft tracking, head moving, VOR on
- **Feet:** Front foot steps slightly across; back hip loads; feel glute and lat stretch on hitting side
- **Body:** Bigger shoulder turn than forehand; non-dominant hand at throat pulls racket back — feel hip-to-lat-to-elbow stretch; elbow stays high, above shoulder
- **Racket:** Edge leads, never the face; face closes slightly, points toward back fence

## Park Phase: At the Bounce
- **Head:** Stops but stays side-on; chin over front shoulder — don't turn chin toward net
- **Eyes:** Jump to contact zone — further to side and higher than forehand, in front of front foot; lock; say "park"
- **Proprioception check:** Without looking, feel pinky-side edge of hand leading, lat stretched long — like holding a saber above shoulder

## Execution: The Saber Draw
- **Fascial recoil:** Hips start forward while racket still travels back (counter-movement loads elastic energy); lat pulls elbow down and forward like drawing sword from shoulder sheath at 45°
- **Feel:** Edge slices down behind ball, face squares at last instant — high-to-low-to-high; slice down to clear net with topspin
- **Tension:** Upper back firm at 5/10, hand at 3/10 until contact → 6/10; wrist in firm extension, never floppy
- **Face:** Eastern backhand grip runs vertical-to-slightly-closed at side-on contact point
- **Brake:** Chest stays side-on — never let it open toward net; braking the chest makes the saber snap

## Hold Phase: After Contact
- Eyes and head still side-on; see contact zone, not opponent; chin over shoulder until ball crosses net
- Finish: racket high, elbow high, chest still sideways; facing net at finish = head moved early, lost back line

## Fault Signals (1H Backhand)

| Symptom | Root Cause | Fix |
|---------|------------|-----|
| Balls go long | Chest opened early, opening face | Hold side-on longer, keep chin over shoulder |
| Balls into net | Contact too late/low for high ball | Take earlier, further in front, higher elbow |
| No spin/pace | Face opened at contact — edge didn't lead | Higher elbow, edge leading, drop racket behind |
| Shoulder pain | Muscled with arm, no lat stretch | Non-dominant hand pulls racket back — feel lat stretch |
| Shanks | Chin turned to net, head turned, shoulder opened | Chin over shoulder until ball crosses net |

## Two-Handed Backhand Hybrid Map

| Variable | Name | Two-Handed Implementation |
|----------|------|---------------------------|
| Q | Quiet Eye | Eyes park at contact zone (side + front), chin over front shoulder |
| V | Vestibular | Head stays side-on, chin over front shoulder through contact |
| ROOT | Rooting | Wide base, both feet thực on load; front foot root holds side-on position |
| 8 | Figure-8 | Vertical 8: high entry behind, crossover drop, up-and-forward exit |
| 45° | Plane | Diagonal glass — high behind, low in front; edge leads, face squares late |
| Brake | Impulse | Chest brakes side-on; non-dominant hand pushes, dominant hand guides edge |
| Pulse | Fa jin | Both hands snap together — dominant pushes through, non-dominant guides edge |
| Jin | Kình | Ting feels stretch both sides; Hua absorbs high ball; Fa = double-hand snap |

## Grip Architecture
- **Bottom hand (dominant):** Eastern or semi-western forehand grip — push engine, generates pace; 3/10 → 6/10 at contact
- **Top hand (non-dominant):** Eastern or continental backhand grip — saber guide, controls face and edge; constant 4/10 locks face

## Execution: Double-Hand Sync
- **Fascia:** Hips start forward while racket still drops — counter-movement loads both lines
- **Push hand (bottom):** Chest fires, palm pushes through like forehand; 3/10 → 6/10
- **Guide hand (top):** Lat pulls elbow down, edge leads, face squares at last instant; constant 4/10
- **Sync:** Bottom hand pushes through while top hand guides edge — arrive together at contact
- **Brake:** Chest brakes side-on, front foot root holds; both hands snap — bottom pushes, top guides

## Fault Signals (2H Backhand)

| Symptom | Root Cause | Fix |
|---------|------------|-----|
| Balls go wide/long | Top hand opened face | Constant 4/10 on top hand, guiding edge |
| Balls into net | Bottom hand didn't push through | Bottom hand runs 3/10 → 6/10 at contact |
| No power | Hands fighting each other | Drill one hand alone, then combine |
| Late contact | Takeback too big for ball speed | Compact takeback, non-dominant hand leads |
| Shoulder pain | Dominant hand muscling, non-dominant slack | Non-dominant hand leads takeback, dominant follows |

## Drills: Integrating the Two Hands
1. **Bottom hand only:** 20 balls — feel push engine
2. **Top hand only:** 20 balls — feel saber guide
3. **Alternating:** 1 bottom, 1 top, 1 both — 30 balls
4. **Sync drill:** Partner feeds high balls; "bottom pushes, top guides" — 20 balls

## Pre-Point Reset (Two-Handed)
1. Feet: wide, both tripods active; feel tear between feet
2. Head: side-on, chin over front shoulder, non-dominant hand on throat
3. Hands: bottom = push (3/10), top = guide (constant 4/10)

## Checklist
**GLUTE. LAT. BOTH HANDS. HEAD SIDE-ON. EYES PARK. PUSH PLUS GUIDE.**
- Pre-point: wide base, both tripods, non-dominant hand on throat, roles clear
- Load: big unit turn, non-dominant hand pulls racket back, feel hip-to-lat stretch both sides
- Park: head stops side-on, chin over shoulder; eyes jump to contact zone 30cm front+side, "park"
- Execute: hips start, bottom pushes through, top guides edge, arrive together
- Hold: eyes/head stay side-on; chin over shoulder until ball crosses net
- Balls wide = top hand opened face; balls net = bottom hand didn't push through
