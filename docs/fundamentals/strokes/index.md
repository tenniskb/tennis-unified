# Strokes Overview

Comprehensive guide to tennis strokes: forehand, backhand, serve, return, volley, overhead, slice, drop shot & lob, and approach shots.

---

## Stroke Systems

### [Forehand](forehand/index.md)
**Forehand Push System** — The body pushes the hand, the hand pushes the racket. A ground-up chain: root → V → Q → 8 → 45° → brake → pulse.

### [Backhand](backhand/index.md)
**Saber (1H) & Hybrid (2H)** — The edge leads, face squares last. Non-dominant hand guides; dominant hand pushes. Covers both one-handed saber and two-handed hybrid systems.

### [Serve](serve/index.md)
**Isolated Kinetic Chain** — The only shot you control completely. Continental grip, trophy position loads SSC, 1-inch pronation pulse.

### [Return of Serve](return-of-serve/index.md)
**The Reactive Art** — No time to think. Short takeback, Hua Jin, block and redirect. Quiet Eye is everything.

### [Volley](volley/index.md)
**The Compact Game** — No takeback. Block, absorb, 1-inch pulse. Continental grip, firm wrist, step across on diagonal.

### [Overhead](overhead/index.md)
**The Serve You Hit in Play** — Same kinetic chain as serve. Turn sideways, crossover steps, let gravity drop racket, brake and snap.

### [Slice](slice/index.md)
**The Rhythm Disruptor** — Edge leads, face follows. Constant firmness (4-5/10), high-to-low path, Hua Jin absorb and redirect.

### [Drop Shot & Lob](drop-shot-lob/index.md)
**Touch & Disruption** — Drop shot = disguised drive (3-1 drill). Offensive lob = topspin brush up at 45°. All touch shots = Hua Jin.

### [Approach Shot](approach-shot/index.md)
**Transition Game** — Hit and move forward. Deep approach, directional control, immediate split step at opponent's contact.
