# Forehand Fundamentals

Comprehensive technical guide to the forehand push system — the body pushing the hand, the hand pushing the racket.

## The Forehand Push System Map

| Variable | Name | Forehand Push Implementation |
|----------|------|------------------------------|
| Q | Quiet Eye | Eyes park on the contact zone, ~30cm in front of front foot, waist height |
| V | Vestibular | Head dead still, chin tucked, VOR quiet from bounce through contact hold |
| ROOT | Rooting | Back foot screws in on load; front foot screws hard on brake |
| 8 | Figure-8 | Horizontal 8 on 45° glass; crossover inside body = amortization phase |
| 45° | Plane | Diagonal glass — low behind, high in front; push through and up |
| Brake | Impulse | Front leg/hip stops hard; torso stops, arm whips through — 1-inch pulse |
| Pulse | Fa jin | Palm pushes through ball 1 inch; never a swing — a push |
| Jin | Kình | Ting feels spiral stretch; Hua absorbs pace; Fa releases pulse |

## Load Phase: As the Ball Leaves the Opponent
- **Eyes:** Soft tracking, head moving, VOR on
- **Feet:** Back foot (right for right-hander) grips inside edge; feel arch lift
- **Hips:** Turning, stretch across belly from back hip to front ribs (spiral line loading)
- **Racket:** Chest-led takeback (never arm-led); elbow in front of body line; wrist loose; butt cap points side fence

## Park Phase: At the Bounce
- **Head:** Stops dead still; vestibular quiet (VOR quiet)
- **Eyes:** Jump to contact zone — 30-45cm in front of front foot, waist height; lock; say "park"
- **Body:** Knees bend to bring contact zone up to waist (not hands dropping to ball); keeps fascial line long
- **Proprioception check:** Without looking at racket, feel palm facing side fence behind racket

## Execution: The Forward Swing
- **Fascial recoil:** Chest fires first, pulls arm — sequence: oblique → chest → palm; arm is a rope
- **Feel:** Heel of palm pushes through 12 inches of ball; not up, not across — through (a push)
- **Tension:** 3/10 → 6/10 at contact (30ms) → 3/10; forearm never tightens
- **Face:** Semi-western grip closes face slightly (correct for inside position); never correct with wrist
- **Brake:** Front leg straightens and stomps; front hip hits wall; obliques stop torso rotation
- **Snap:** Torso stopped → arm/racket whip through; palm-push becomes 1-inch pulse

## Hold Phase: After Contact
- Eyes and head stay parked on contact spot; hear ball hit before seeing result; count "through"
- Front foot inside arch still carries pressure; falling back = pulled with arm instead of pushing with spiral line

## Fault Signals

| Symptom | Root Cause | Fix |
|---------|------------|-----|
| Balls go long | Head/eyes peeked early, opening face | Park earlier, hold longer |
| Balls into net | Late contact closes face | Contact further in front, start swing earlier |
| No power | Tightened too early, killed SSC | 3/10 takeback, loose wrist drop |
| Elbow pain | Tight shoulder broke spiral line | Loose arm — let chest pull arm |
| Inconsistent contact | No Quiet Eye, contact drifts | Park at bounce, hold through contact |
| Shanks | Head moves, VOR fires, face twists | Keep head still, eyes parked |

## Pre-Point Reset (5 Seconds)
1. Feet to ground: lift toes, press tripod (big toe, little toe, heel); feel inside arch engage
2. Head to level: chin tucked, eyes level; slow head turn L/R; vestibular settles
3. Hands to 3/10: squeeze to 8/10, drop to 3/10; wiggle fingers; forearm soft

## Quick-Scan Card
**ARCH → OBLIQUE → HEAD → PARK → PUSH**

## Checklist
**FEET SCREW. HEAD STILL. EYES PARK. SMALL 8 ON 45°. BRAKE. PULSE.**
- Pre-point: tripod feet, level head, 3/10 hands
- Load: back foot screws in, oblique stretches, chest opens, wrist loose
- Park: head stops, eyes jump to contact zone 30cm front, say "park"
- Execute: chest fires first, palm pushes 12 inches, front leg brakes
- Hold: eyes/head stay parked until ball crosses net
- Bicep burn/elbow sting = line broke at shoulder; reset: back foot screws, chest pushes hand
