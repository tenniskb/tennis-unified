# The Mechanic and the Magician

### The Mechanic

### and The Magician

### Paul Annacone

What type of player are you? A Mechanic? A Magician? Or some combination
of the two\--like Roger Federer. At Indian Wells John Yandell had a
chance to sit down with arguably the leading and most successful coach
on tour, Paul Annacone. Listen to this fascinating analysis of the two
major types of players, how to coach them, and the surprising
implications for junior development. Another Tennisplayer exclusive!
