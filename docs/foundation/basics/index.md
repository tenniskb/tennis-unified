# Foundation — Basics

The entry point. The work every other level of tennis assumes you've already done.

## Why This Section Exists

The basics have layers. What you couldn't feel last year, you feel clearly now. What you feel now, you'll feel differently next year. The best coaches I respect spend half their practice time on what's below.

## How to Use This

Each Basics article is structured the same way:

1. What it is — plain-language definition
2. Why it matters — the failure modes if you skip it
3. How to practice it — specific drills you can do on court
4. What it feels like when it's right
5. Common mistakes — and the cue to fix them

Start at the top. Don't skip. Grip is the most boring and most important section here. Everything else gets harder if your grip is unclear.

## A Note for 50+ Players

Many of these basics include slice variations built for older bodies. Slice isn't a compromise. For 50+ players, it's often the highest-percentage option on any given ball. If you're 50+, train the slice variation alongside the topspin variation — they're not a beginner version of the real thing. They're the real thing, in a different shape.
