# The trophy position

**Serve Secrets:**

**The Trophy Position**

**Jeff Salzenstein**

In this third and final article in his serve series, Jeff Salzenstein
talks about the importance of the trophy position, and the most common
errors players make trying to reach it. Then he introduces an original
new drill to help you find it perfectly for yourself. Integrated with
his previous articles on the starting stance and the arm action for
spin, this article helps any player create a blueprint for high level
serving.

We're excited to have back Jeff as a Tennisplayer contributor!
