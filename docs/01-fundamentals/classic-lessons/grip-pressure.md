# Grip Pressure

### Grip Pressure

### John Craig

Technical patterns are key to every stroke. But the right muscle tension
is as well. \"Feel the racket head.\" It\'s a simple concept that solves
a question on all the strokes---how tight should you hold the grip? Too
loose, you lose control. Too tight you restrict the swing and tighten
up, risking injury.

Check out John's solution!

John is a former Division I Collegiate player with over 35 years of
playing, coaching and teaching experience. He is an Elite member of the
