# The Golden Move # 6 Low Spin

**Golden Move 6:**

# Low Spin

**David Bailey**

Renowned footwork expert David Bailey continues his incredible series on
the 12 Golden Moves for world class footwork, this time teaching you the
sixth move---Low Spin. The Low Spin move, usually hit semi-open, creates
power by aggressive hip spin. In this segment David shows the key to
hitting it both down the line and crosscourt by controlling the angle of
the outside foot.
