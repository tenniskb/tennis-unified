# Half Volleying

The half volley is hit immediately after the ball bounces - a "short hop." It's a defensive shot requiring excellent timing and soft hands.

---

## Technique

### 1. Recognition
- **Identify** ball will land at your feet
- **Don't back up** - stay in position
- **Prepare early** - racket down low

### 2. The Swing
- **Minimal backswing** - racket already low
- **Open face** - lift the ball
- **Short, compact** motion
- **Meet the ball** right after bounce

### 3. Contact
- **Right after bounce** (1-2 inches)
- **Racket face open** (45 degrees)
- **Low contact point** - knee level or lower
- **Soft hands** - absorb pace

### 4. Follow-through
- **Guide the ball** up and deep
- **Finish high** - racket up
- **Weight forward** - step into it

---

## Key Points

| Element | Key |
|---------|-----|
| **Early recognition** | Don't be surprised |
| **Racket low early** | No time for big backswing |
| **Soft hands** | Absorb, don't hit |
| **Open face** | Lift over net |
| **Deep placement** | Push opponent back |

---

## When You Hit Half Volleys

- **Opponent hits at your feet**
- **Service return** - fast serve at feet
- **Approach shot** - low dipping ball
- **Volley exchange** - ball drops at feet
- **Defensive** - stretched wide, low ball

---

## Common Errors

| Error | Result | Fix |
|-------|--------|-----|
| **Late recognition** | Jammed, mishit | Watch opponent's contact |
| **Backing up** | Worse position | Stay, take on rise |
| **Closed face** | Into net | Open face 45° |
| **Hard swing** | Long, no control | Soft hands, guide |
| **No follow-through** | Short, sits up | Finish high, deep |

---

## Practice Drills

1. **Short hop feed** - coach feeds at feet
2. **Service return** - fast serves at body/feet
3. **Volley-to-half-volley** - partner hits low
4. **Wall practice** - hit against wall, take short hops
5. **Point play** - force half volley situations

---

## Pro Tip

**The half volley is a "save the point" shot** - your goal is to neutralize and get back in the rally. Hit it deep to the middle to buy time and recover position.
