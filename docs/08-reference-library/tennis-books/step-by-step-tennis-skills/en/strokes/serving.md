# Serving

The serve is the only stroke you have complete control over. It starts every point and can be a major weapon.

---

## Grip

**Continental grip** (hammer grip) - used for all serve types:
- Index knuckle on bevel 2
- Heel pad on bevel 1
- Allows flat, slice, and kick serves

---

## Stance

### Platform Stance
- Feet stay in position throughout
- More stable, consistent
- Better for beginners

### Pinpoint Stance
- Back foot moves up to front foot
- More explosive, more power
- Advanced players

---

## The Toss

Critical for consistency:
- **Placement**: slightly in front, at 1 o'clock (righty)
- **Height**: contact at full extension
- **Release**: fingers, not palm
- **Consistency**: same every time

---

## Serve Motion Phases

### 1. Preparation
- Feet set, weight balanced
- Racket and ball together
- Visualize target

### 2. Toss & Backswing
- Toss goes up, racket goes back
- **Trophy position**: racket behind head, elbow up
- Weight on back foot
- Knees bent

### 3. Loading
- Deep knee bend (coil)
- Hip shoulder separation
- Racket drops behind back

### 4. Explosion
- Drive up from legs
- Hips rotate first
- Shoulders follow
- Arm extends upward

### 5. Contact
- **Full extension** at highest point
- **Racket face** square to target (flat)
- **Brush up** for kick, **side** for slice
- **Wrist snap** through contact

### 6. Follow-through
- Racket finishes across body
- Land on front foot
- Quick recovery to ready

---

## Serve Types

| Serve | Grip | Toss | Contact | Use |
|-------|------|------|---------|-----|
| **Flat** | Continental | 12-1 o'clock | Square, max power | First serve, fast courts |
| **Slice** | Continental | 12-1 o'clock | Brush right side | Wide serves, deuce court |
| **Kick/Topspin** | Continental | 11-12 o'clock | Brush up back | Second serve, high bounce |
| **Body** | Continental | 12 o'clock | Into body | Jam returner |

---

## Common Errors

| Error | Result | Fix |
|-------|--------|-----|
| **Toss inconsistent** | All serves off | Practice toss alone |
| **No knee bend** | No power | Deep coil, drive up |
| **Arm only** | Weak, injury risk | Use kinetic chain |
| **Late contact** | Net/long | Toss further forward |
| **No pronation** | No spin/speed | Throw edge of racket |

---

## Practice Drills

1. **Toss practice** - 50 tosses daily, catch on racket
2. **Shadow serves** - full motion, no ball
3. **Target practice** - cones in service boxes
4. **Second serve drill** - 20 kick serves in a row
5. **Point play** - serve + 1, practice patterns
