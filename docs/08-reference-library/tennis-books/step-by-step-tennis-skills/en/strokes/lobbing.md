# Lobbing

The lob is a high, arcing shot hit over the opponent's head when they are at the net. It can be defensive (buying time) or offensive (winning the point).

---

## Types of Lobs

| Type | Purpose | Trajectory | Spin |
|------|---------|------------|------|
| **Defensive** | Buy time, push opponent back | Very high, deep | Topspin or none |
| **Offensive** | Win point over net player | Lower, faster | Heavy topspin |
| **Topspin lob** | Aggressive, dips in | High arc, sharp drop | Heavy topspin |

---

## Technique

### 1. Preparation
- Recognize opponent at net early
- Unit turn (same as groundstroke)
- Racket back **lower** than normal
- Open racket face

### 2. The Swing
- **Extreme low-to-high** path (60-80 degrees)
- **Brush up** back of ball aggressively
- **Open face** at contact (45+ degrees)
- **Full extension** upward

### 3. Contact
- **Ball height**: comfortable, often lower
- **Racket face**: very open
- **Swing path**: nearly vertical
- **Contact point**: slightly in front

### 4. Follow-through
- Racket finishes **high overhead**
- **Weight back** (defensive) or **forward** (offensive)
- **Eyes track ball** upward

---

## Offensive vs Defensive Lob

| Element | Defensive | Offensive |
|---------|-----------|-----------|
| **Urgency** | High (pressed) | Low (time) |
| **Height** | Maximum | High but controlled |
| **Depth** | Baseline | Service line to baseline |
| **Spin** | Light topspin | Heavy topspin |
| **Follow-through** | High, abbreviated | Full, over shoulder |
| **Recovery** | Scramble back | Move forward/hold ground |

---

## Common Errors

| Error | Result | Fix |
|-------|--------|-----|
| **Not enough height** | Smashed back | Swing more vertically |
| **Too much height** | Lands short | Flatten slightly, more pace |
| **No spin** | Floats, easy smash | Heavy topspin brush |
| **Late recognition** | Rushed, poor contact | Read opponent earlier |
| **Wrong grip** | Can't open face | Continental/Eastern |

---

## When to Lob

- **Opponent at net** - primary situation
- **Opponent poor overhead** - exploit weakness
- **You're stretched wide** - defensive lob buys time
- **Change pace** - disrupt rhythm
- **Windy conditions** - wind helps/lifts

---

## Practice Drills

1. **Lob vs smash** - partner at net, you lob
2. **Target lobs** - hit to deep corners
3. **Topspin lob drill** - from baseline, over net player
4. **Defensive lob** - on the run, high and deep
5. **Point play** - must lob to win point
