# Ground Strokes

The ground stroke is the fundamental stroke in tennis. When executing a ground stroke, you should focus on the following elements:

---

## 1. Ready Position

Stand in the "ready" position with knees slightly bent and the racket out in front of your body.

- Feet shoulder-width apart
- Weight on balls of feet
- Knees flexed
- Racket centered, head up
- Non-dominant hand on throat

---

## 2. Swing Motion

Use a smooth, efficient swing motion from low to high.

- **Unit turn** - shoulders and hips rotate together
- **Racket back** - take racket back early, level with contact point
- **Drop** - racket head drops below ball level
- **Swing forward** - accelerate from low to high
- **Contact** - in front of body, waist high
- **Follow-through** - over opposite shoulder

---

## 3. Contact Point

Hit the ball at the optimal height (usually at hip level) with the racket at a slightly closed angle.

- **Height**: waist to hip level
- **Distance**: comfortably in front of front foot
- **Racket face**: slightly closed (topspin) or vertical (flat)
- **Eyes**: focused on contact point

---

## 4. Follow-through

Complete the stroke smoothly with the racket finishing over the opposite shoulder and prepare for the next shot.

- Racket wraps around neck/shoulder
- Weight fully on front foot
- Back foot comes around
- Hips and shoulders face net
- Quick recovery to ready position

---

## Common Errors

| Error | Correction |
|-------|------------|
| **Late preparation** | Turn shoulders earlier, racket back sooner |
| **Hitting late** | Contact further in front, better timing |
| **Open racket face** | Close face slightly, brush up |
| **No follow-through** | Finish over shoulder every time |
| **Off balance** | Wider base, better weight transfer |

---

## Practice Drills

1. **Shadow swings** - practice motion without ball
2. **Drop feed** - coach feeds from hand, focus on contact
3. **Rally practice** - cooperative rally, consistency focus
4. **Target practice** - hit to specific zones
5. **Ball machine** - groove stroke under pressure
