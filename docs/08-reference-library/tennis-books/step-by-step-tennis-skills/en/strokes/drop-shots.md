# Drop Shots

The drop shot is a softly hit ball that barely clears the net and lands short in the opponent's court. It requires touch, disguise, and perfect timing.

---

## Technique

### 1. Preparation (Disguise)
- **Look like a normal groundstroke**
- Same unit turn, same backswing
- **Don't telegraph** the shot

### 2. The Swing
- **Shorten** the backswing at last moment
- **Open racket face** significantly
- **Soft hands** - relax grip
- **Slice under** the ball gently

### 3. Contact
- **Ball at comfortable height**
- **Racket face open** (45+ degrees)
- **Brush under** with minimal pace
- **Contact slightly in front**

### 4. Follow-through
- **Minimal** - just guide the ball
- **Racket finishes** high and open
- **Stay balanced** for next shot

---

## Key Elements

| Element | Description |
|---------|-------------|
| **Disguise** | Look like drive until contact |
| **Touch** | Feather the ball, soft hands |
| **Placement** | Just over net, near sideline |
| **Spin** | Backspin/sidespin to die |
| **Timing** | Opponent deep or moving back |

---

## When to Drop Shot

| Situation | Why |
|-----------|-----|
| **Opponent deep** | Must run far forward |
| **Opponent slow** | Can't reach in time |
| **After deep rally** | Caught behind baseline |
| **Change of pace** | Disrupt rhythm |
| **Opponent at net** | Wrong - they'll get it! |

---

## Common Errors

| Error | Result | Fix |
|-------|--------|-----|
| **Telegraphing** | Opponent reads it | Same prep as drive |
| **Too much pace** | Bounces high, attackable | Soften hands, less swing |
| **Too high over net** | Easy get | Lower trajectory |
| **Wrong time** | Opponent crushes it | Only when deep/back |
| **No spin** | Sits up | Slice under for backspin |

---

## Practice Drills

1. **Drop shot targets** - cones near net
2. **Disguise drill** - alternate drive/drop
3. **Point play** - must drop shot to win
4. **Two-ball drill** - drive then drop
5. **Touch game** - mini-tennis, drops only

---

## Pro Tip

**The best drop shots come after a deep, heavy topspin shot** that pushes your opponent 3-4 feet behind the baseline. They're committed to moving back, making the forward sprint nearly impossible.
