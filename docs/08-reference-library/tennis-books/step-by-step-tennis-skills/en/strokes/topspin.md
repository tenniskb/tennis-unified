# Topspin

Topspin is created by brushing up the back of the ball with a low-to-high swing path. It causes the ball to dip sharply and bounce high.

---

## Technique

### 1. Preparation
- Early unit turn (shoulders + hips)
- Racket back at shoulder height
- Non-dominant hand on throat
- Weight on back foot

### 2. The Swing
- **Drop the racket head** below the ball (6-12 inches)
- **Brush up** the back of the ball
- **Low-to-high** swing path (45-60 degrees)
- **Racket face** slightly closed at contact
- **Contact point** in front, waist high

### 3. Contact
- **Ball height**: waist to chest level
- **Racket path**: upward 45-60 degrees
- **Racket face**: 10-15 degrees closed
- **Sweet spot**: center of strings

### 4. Follow-through
- Racket finishes **over opposite shoulder**
- **Elbow points** at target
- **Weight fully transferred** to front foot
- **Shoulders face net**

---

## Key Differences from Flat Groundstroke

| Element | Flat | Topspin |
|---------|------|---------|
| **Swing path** | Level | Low to high (45-60°) |
| **Racket face** | Vertical | Slightly closed |
| **Contact** | Middle of ball | Lower half, brush up |
| **Finish** | Across body | Over shoulder |
| **Trajectory** | Flat | High arc, sharp dip |
| **Bounce** | Low, skidding | High, kicking |

---

## Common Errors

| Error | Result | Fix |
|-------|--------|-----|
| **Racket face too open** | Ball sails long | Close face 10-15° |
| **Not enough brush** | Flat ball, no dip | Drop racket head more |
| **Late contact** | Weak shot, no spin | Meet ball further front |
| **Short follow-through** | Inconsistent spin | Finish over shoulder |
| **Arm only swing** | No power/spin | Use legs, hips, torso |

---

## When to Use Topspin

- **Rally balls** - high percentage, deep
- **Passing shots** - dip at net player's feet
- **High balls** - control the bounce
- **Clay courts** - high bounce troubles opponents
- **Defense** - buys time to recover

---

## Practice Drills

1. **Wall practice** - hit topspin against wall, catch bounce
2. **Mini-tennis** - short court, extreme brush
3. **Crosscourt rally** - depth + spin consistency
4. **Target zones** - deep corners with height
5. **Ball machine** - groove the swing path
