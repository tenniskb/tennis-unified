# Slicing

The slice (underspin) is created by cutting under the ball with a high-to-low swing path. It produces a low, skidding bounce.

---

## Technique

### 1. Preparation
- Early unit turn
- Racket **above** contact point
- Continental or Eastern backhand grip
- Weight on back foot

### 2. The Swing
- **High-to-low** swing path (30-45 degrees down)
- **Open racket face** (45 degrees to sky)
- **Cut under** the ball
- **Firm wrist** - don't break at contact

### 3. Contact
- **Ball height**: varies (knee to chest)
- **Racket path**: downward 30-45 degrees
- **Racket face**: open (45°)
- **Contact point**: slightly in front, to the side

### 4. Follow-through
- Racket finishes **low and across body**
- **Knuckles up** (continental grip)
- **Weight transfers** forward
- **Stay sideways** longer

---

## Types of Slices

| Type | Purpose | Technique |
|------|---------|-----------|
| **Defensive** | Buy time, neutralize | High-to-low, deep, floating |
| **Approach** | Move to net | Lower, penetrating, skidding |
| **Drop shot** | Winner/force error | Extreme touch, barely over net |
| **Changing pace** | Disrupt rhythm | Mix with topspin |

---

## Key Differences from Topspin

| Element | Topspin | Slice |
|---------|---------|-------|
| **Swing path** | Low to high | High to low |
| **Racket face** | Closed | Open |
| **Spin** | Forward | Backward |
| **Trajectory** | High arc | Low, flat |
| **Bounce** | High, kicking | Low, skidding |
| **Grip** | Semi-Western/Western | Continental/Eastern |

---

## Common Errors

| Error | Result | Fix |
|-------|--------|-----|
| **Wrist breaks** | Floats long | Firm wrist, knuckles up |
| **Too much chop** | Ball in net | Shallower path |
| **Late contact** | Pops up | Meet ball earlier |
| **No body turn** | Weak, arm-only | Unit turn shoulders |
| **Wrong grip** | Can't open face | Continental/Eastern |

---

## When to Use Slice

- **Low balls** - easier to get under
- **Wide balls** - stretch and slice back
- **Approach shots** - low bounce at net
- **Change of pace** - disrupt opponent's rhythm
- **Defense** - buy time to recover
- **Grass/fast courts** - skids through

---

## Practice Drills

1. **Slice rally** - cooperative, consistency
2. **Approach + volley** - slice approach, move in
3. **Drop shot practice** - touch and feel
4. **Mixed rally** - alternate topspin/slice
5. **Target practice** - deep corners, low trajectory
