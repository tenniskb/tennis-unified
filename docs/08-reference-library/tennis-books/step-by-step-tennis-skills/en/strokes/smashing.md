# Smashing

The overhead smash is the put-away shot for high balls. It's similar to the serve motion but hit on the move.

---

## Technique

### 1. Recognition & Turn
- **Immediate recognition** of lob
- **Turn sideways** instantly (unit turn)
- **Point** at ball with non-dominant hand
- **Backpedal** or side-shuffle

### 2. Positioning
- **Get behind** the ball
- **Adjust with small steps**
- **Weight on back foot**
- **Racket up early** (trophy position)

### 3. The Swing
- **Same as serve** - throw motion
- **Drive up** from legs
- **Hip/shoulder rotation**
- **Full extension** at contact

### 4. Contact
- **Highest comfortable point**
- **Slightly in front** of body
- **Racket face** square to target
- **Wrist snap** through ball

### 5. Follow-through
- Racket across body
- Land on front foot
- **Move forward** to net position

---

## Key Points

| Element | Key |
|---------|-----|
| **Early turn** | Sideways immediately |
| **Point at ball** | Tracks ball, keeps shoulders turned |
| **Get behind ball** | Don't reach back |
| **Trophy position** | Racket up, elbow up |
| **Leg drive** | Power from ground up |
| **Contact high** | Full extension |

---

## Common Errors

| Error | Result | Fix |
|-------|--------|-----|
| **Late turn** | Off balance, weak | Turn instantly on lob |
| **Backing up straight** | Can't move forward | Side-shuffle, crossover |
| **Not getting behind** | Reaching, mishit | Move feet, get under |
| **Dropping racket** | Late contact | Keep racket up |
| **No leg drive** | Arm only, weak | Bend knees, explode up |
| **Looking down early** | Miss contact | Watch ball to strings |

---

## Smash Variations

| Type | Situation | Technique |
|------|-----------|-----------|
| **Standard** | High, floating lob | Full power, flat |
| **Angle smash** | Short lob | Sharp crosscourt |
| **Jump smash** | Very high lob | Jump, hit at max height |
| **Backhand smash** | Lob to backhand side | Two-handed or one-handed |
| **Defensive smash** | Deep, difficult lob | Controlled placement |

---

## Practice Drills

1. **Lob-smash drill** - partner lobs, you smash
2. **Movement drill** - side-to-side smashes
3. **Target smash** - hit to specific zones
4. **Backhand smash** - practice weak side
5. **Point play** - lob/smash game

---

## Safety Note

**Never backpedal straight backwards** - high risk of falling. Use crossover steps or side-shuffle to move backwards while staying sideways.
