# Take-off and Running

Speed to the ball starts with an explosive first move and efficient running mechanics.

---

## The First Move (Take-off)

### Key Elements
1. **Split step lands** → immediate push
2. **Push off outside foot** (foot away from ball)
3. **Drive hips** in direction of ball
4. **Shoulders turn** simultaneously
5. **Racket prepares** during movement

### Timing
- **Ideal**: Split step lands exactly as opponent contacts ball
- **Early**: Wait on ground, lose explosiveness
- **Late**: Reaction delayed, rushed movement

---

## Running Technique

### Acceleration Phase (0-3 steps)
- **Forward lean** from ankles
- **Drive knees** up and forward
- **Pump arms** in opposition to legs
- **Short, powerful** strides

### Top Speed Phase
- **Upright posture** (slight lean)
- **Full extension** of back leg
- **High knee** lift
- **Relaxed shoulders** and arms

### Deceleration/Adjustment
- **Shorten strides** approaching ball
- **Widen base** for balance
- **Lower center** of gravity
- **Racket ready** for swing

---

## Movement Patterns

### Lateral (Side-to-Side)
- **Side-shuffle** for short distances
- **Crossover step** for longer distances
- **Hips square** to net (mostly)
- **Push off** outside foot

### Forward/Backward
- **Forward**: Natural sprint mechanics
- **Backward**: Crossover steps, not backpedal
- **Never** run straight backwards - fall risk

### Diagonal
- **Most common** in tennis
- **45-degree angle** typically
- **Combination** of lateral + forward

---

## Court Coverage Drills

### 1. Spider Drill
- Center mark → each corner → back to center
- Touch each corner with racket
- Time and improve

### 2. Suicide Sprints
- Baseline → service line → baseline → net → baseline
- Touch each line
- Build tennis-specific endurance

### 3. Random Feed
- Ball machine/partner feeds random
- Focus on first move explosiveness
- Practice all movement patterns

### 3. Shadow Movement
- No ball, perfect technique
- Visualize ball, move perfectly
- Build muscle memory

---

## Speed vs Quickness

| Quality | Definition | Tennis Application |
|---------|------------|-------------------|
| **Speed** | Max velocity straight line | Chasing lobs, wide balls |
| **Quickness** | Acceleration, change of direction | First move, recovery, adjustment |

**Tennis needs QUICKNESS more than speed** - short bursts, constant direction changes.

---

## Common Errors

| Error | Consequence | Fix |
|-------|-------------|-----|
| **False step** | Wasted time | Push off outside foot only |
| **High center** | Slow direction change | Stay low, knees bent |
| **Arms crossing** | Rotation, imbalance | Pump straight, opposition |
| **Heel striking** | Braking, slow | Forefoot/midfoot strike |
| **No split step** | Late first move | Split step EVERY ball |

---

## Fitness for Tennis Movement

- **Plyometrics** - explosive power
- **Lateral bounds** - side-to-side power
- **Sprints** (10-20m) - acceleration
- **Agility ladder** - foot speed, coordination
- **Core strength** - stability during movement

---

## Practice Progression

1. **Technique** - shadow, perfect form
2. **Speed** - max effort, short distance
3. **Reaction** - partner/coach signals
4. **Game-like** - random feeds, decisions
5. **Match** - apply under pressure
