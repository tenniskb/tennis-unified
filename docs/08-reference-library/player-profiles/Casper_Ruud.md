# Casper Ruud

## Overview
- **Full Name**: Casper Ruud
- **Date of Birth**: December 22, 1998
- **Nationality**: Norwegian
- **Turned Pro**: 2015
- **Plays**: Right-handed (two-handed backhand)
- **Highest Ranking**: No. 2 (achieved September 2022)
- **Career Titles**: 10 ATP singles titles
- **Grand Slam Titles**: 0 (best: finalist 2022 French Open, 2022 US Open, 2023 French Open)
- **Career Prize Money**: Over $15 million

## Playing Style & Strengths
**Clay Court Specialist**: Ruud's game is built for clay, with heavy topspin forehand and exceptional sliding ability.

**Heavy Topspin Forehand**: His forehand generates massive topspin, allowing him to control points from the baseline on slower surfaces.

**Two-Handed Backhand**: Solid and reliable, capable of handling high-bouncing balls and generating pace when needed.

**Varied Serve**: While not a major weapon, his serve is consistent and he can vary placement effectively.

**Mental Toughness**: Shown ability to win big matches, including coming back from deficits in Grand Slam finals.

**Weaknesses**: Can struggle on faster surfaces where his heavy topspin is less effective; movement, while good on clay, is not elite on all courts; second serve can be vulnerable.

## Career Highlights
- **2022**: Reached French Open final, losing to Rafael Nadal in four sets.
- **2022**: Reached US Open final, losing to Carlos Alcaraz in four sets.
- **2023**: Reached French Open final, losing to Novak Djokovic in straight sets.
- **2021**: Won ATP 500 title in Geneva.
- **2020**: Won ATP 250 titles in Geneva and Kitzbühel.
- **2021**: Represented Norway at the Olympics (singles and doubles).
- **Multiple ATP 250 titles**: Buenos Aires (2020, 2021), Geneva (2021, 2022), etc.
- **Olympic Participant**: Represented Norway at Tokyo 2020 Olympics.

## Rivalries
- **Ruud vs. Nadal**: Several tight matches on clay, with Nadal generally holding the edge.
- **Ruud vs. Alcaraz**: Competitive matches, including the 2022 US Open final.
- **Ruud vs. Djokovic**: Several close matches, often with Djokovic having the edge on faster surfaces.
- **Ruud vs. Tsitsipas**: Several three-set battles, often on clay.

## Legacy (Early Career)
- **Norwegian Tennis Star**: Norway's most successful tennis player in history.
- **Clay Court Master**: Already considered one of the best clay court players of his generation.
- **Grand Slam Finalist**: Reached three Grand Slam finals by age 24, showing his consistency at the highest level.
- **Next Generation Leader**: Part of the group of players pushing the boundaries of men's tennis alongside Alcaraz, Sinner, and Rune.

## Fun Facts & Trivia

- Known for his calm and composed on-court demeanor, rarely showing emotion.
- Avid football fan and supporter of English club Liverpool FC.
- Known for his charitable work, particularly supporting children's causes in Norway.
- Speaks Norwegian and English fluently.
- Has a distinctive celebration where he points to his temple after winning big points.
