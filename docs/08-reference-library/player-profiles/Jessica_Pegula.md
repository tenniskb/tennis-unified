# Jessica Pegula

## Overview
- **Full Name**: Jessica Pegula
- **Date of Birth**: February 24, 1994
- **Nationality**: American
- **Turned Pro**: 2009
- **Plays**: Right-handed (two-handed backhand)
- **Highest Ranking**: No. 3 (achieved September 2022)
- **Career Titles**: 6 WTA singles titles
- **Grand Slam Titles**: 0 (best: quarterfinalist Australian Open 2023, Wimbledon 2022, US Open 2022)
- **Career Prize Money**: Over $10 million

## Playing Style & Strengths
**All-Court Game**: Pegula possesses a solid all-court game, with effective groundstrokes, net play, and serving.

**Two-Handed Backhand**: Her backhand is a reliable weapon, capable of handling pace and generating angles.

**Varied Serve**: While not the biggest server, she can place her serve effectively and mix in spin.

**Mental Toughness**: Shown resilience in big matches, including coming back from deficits.

**Weaknesses**: Can sometimes be inconsistent with her timing; movement, while good, is not elite compared to the very best; second serve can be vulnerable at times.

## Career Highlights

- **Multiple WTA 1000 titles**: Toronto (2022), etc.
