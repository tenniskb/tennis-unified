# Barbora Krejcikova

## Overview
- **Full Name**: Barbora Krejcikova
- **Date of Birth**: December 18, 1995
- **Nationality**: Czech
- **Turned Pro**: 2014
- **Plays**: Right-handed (two-handed backhand)
- **Highest Ranking**: No. 2 (achieved October 2022)
- **Career Titles**: 10 WTA singles titles, 10 WTA doubles titles
- **Grand Slam Titles**: 2 singles (2021 French Open), 6 doubles (various)
- **Career Prize Money**: Over $15 million

## Playing Style & Strengths
**All-Court Versatility**: Krejcikova excels in all aspects of the game, from powerful baseline exchanges to delicate net play and precision serving.

**Excellent Volley and Net Play**: Her doubles background gives her exceptional touch and reflexes at the net, making her a dangerous opponent in short points.

**Varied Serve**: She can serve with power, spin, and placement, keeping opponents off-balance.

**Two-Handed Backhand**: Solid and reliable, capable of handling pace and generating angles.

**Mental Toughness**: Shown resilience in big matches, including coming from behind to win tight sets.

**Weaknesses**: Can sometimes be inconsistent with her groundstroke depth; movement, while good, is not elite compared to the very best.

## Career Highlights
- **2021**: Won French Open singles, defeating Anastasia Pavlyuchenkova in the final.
- **2021**: Won French Open doubles (with Katerina Siniakova).
- **2022**: Won Wimbledon doubles (with Katerina Siniakova).
- **2022**: Won WTA Finals (singles).
- **Multiple WTA 1000 titles**: Doha (2022), etc.
- **Olympic Medalist**: Gold medal in women's doubles at Tokyo 2020 Olympics (with Katerina Siniakova).
- **Former World No. 1 in Doubles**: Held the top doubles ranking for an extended period.

## Rivalries
- **Krejcikova vs. Swiatek**: Competitive matches, often with Swiatek having the edge on clay.
- **Krejcikova vs. Sabalenka**: Several tight matches, often going to three sets.
- **Krejcikova vs. Rybakina**: Contrasting styles, with Krejcikova's variety vs. Rybakina's power.
- **Krejcikova vs. Gauff**: Several three-set battles.

## Legacy (Early Career)
- **Czech Tennis Star**: Czech Republic's most successful tennis player in recent years.
- **Doubles Specialist Turned Singles Pro**: Successfully transitioned from doubles dominance to singles elite.
- **Grand Slam Champion**: Joined the elite group of women's singles Grand Slam champions.
- **Versatile Player**: Demonstrated effectiveness of an all-court game in the modern era.

## Fun Facts & Trivia
- Comes from a sporting family; her father was a footballer and her mother a volleyball player.
- Known for her calm, composed on-court demeanor, rarely showing emotion.
- Avid reader and often shares book recommendations on social media.
- Fluent in Czech and English, with some Russian.
- Known for her charitable work, particularly supporting children's sports programs in the Czech Republic.
