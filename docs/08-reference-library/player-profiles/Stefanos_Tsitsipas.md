# Stefanos Tsitsipas

## Overview
- **Full Name**: Stefanos Tsitsipas
- **Date of Birth**: August 12, 1998
- **Nationality**: Greek
- **Turned Pro**: 2016
- **Plays**: Right-handed (one-handed backhand)
- **Highest Ranking**: No. 3 (achieved August 2021)
- **Career Titles**: 11 ATP singles titles
- **Grand Slam Titles**: 0 (best: finalist 2021 French Open, 2023 Australian Open)
- **Career Prize Money**: Over $25 million

## Playing Style & Strengths
**All-Court Game with Variety**: Tsitsipas possesses a versatile game, capable of attacking from the baseline, coming to net with confidence, and defending with his one-handed backhand slice.

**Excellent One-Handed Backhand**: One of the best one-handed backhands in the modern game, capable of generating both topspin and slice with precision.

**Serve Variety**: Can serve with power, spin, and placement, making his serve difficult to read.

**Net Game**: Comfortable at the net with good volleys and overheads.

**Mental Toughness**: Has shown resilience in big matches, including coming from behind to win.

**Weaknesses**: Can be inconsistent when his timing is off; movement, while good, is not elite compared to the very best; second serve can be vulnerable at times.

## Career Highlights
- **2021**: Reached French Open final, losing to Novak Djokovic in five sets.
- **2023**: Reached Australian Open final, losing to Novak Djokovic in straight sets.
- **2019**: Won ATP Finals, defeating Dominic Thiem in the final.
- **Multiple ATP 500 titles**: Barcelona (2018, 2021, 2022, 2023), Monte Carlo (2021, 2022, 2024).
- **Masters 1000 titles**: Monte Carlo (2021, 2022, 2024), Marseille (2023).
- **2021**: Led Greece to Davis Cup victory (won decisive rubber against Bosnia and Herzegovina).
- **Olympic Participant**: Represented Greece at Tokyo 2020 Olympics.

## Rivalries
- **Tsitsipas vs. Djokovic**: Several tight matches, including the 2021 French Open final and 2023 Australian Open final.
- **Tsitsipas vs. Nadal**: Competitive matches on clay, with Tsitsipas having notable wins over the Spaniard.
- **Tsitsipas vs. Alcaraz**: Emerging rivalry, with several three-set matches.
- **Tsitsipas vs. Medvedev**: Several close matches, often going to three sets.

## Legacy (Early Career)
- **Greek Tennis Icon**: Greece's most successful tennis player in the Open Era.
- **One-Handed Backhand Standard-Bearer**: One of the last top players to effectively wield the one-handed backhand in the modern power-baseline era.
- **All-Court Player**: Demonstrated effectiveness of an all-court game in the modern era.
- **Next Generation Leader**: Part of the group of players pushing the boundaries of men's tennis alongside Alcaraz, Sinner, and Rune.
