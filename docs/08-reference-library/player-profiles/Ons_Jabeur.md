# Ons Jabeur

## Overview
- **Full Name**: Ons Jabeur
- **Date of Birth**: August 28, 1994
- **Nationality**: Tunisian
- **Turned Pro**: 2010
- **Plays**: Right-handed (one-handed backhand)
- **Highest Ranking**: No. 2 (achieved June 2022)
- **Career Titles**: 5 WTA singles titles
- **Grand Slam Titles**: 0 (best: finalist 2022 Wimbledon, 2022 US Open, 2023 Wimbledon)
- **Career Prize Money**: Over $15 million

## Playing Style & Strengths
**Variety and Creativity**: Jabeur is known for her exceptional shot variety, drop shots, and ability to change pace and direction unexpectedly.

**Excellent One-Handed Backhand**: Her one-handed backhand is a weapon, capable of generating both topspin and slice with precision.

**Drop Shot Mastery**: Considered one of the best drop shot artists on tour, using it effectively to bring opponents in and then pass or lob them.

**Tactical Intelligence**: High tennis IQ, excellent at constructing points and exploiting opponent weaknesses.

**Mental Toughness**: Shown resilience in big matches, including coming back from multiple match points down.

**Weaknesses**: Can sometimes be inconsistent with power groundstrokes; movement, while good, is not elite compared to the very best; serve can be inconsistent at times.

## Career Highlights
- **2022**: Reached Wimbledon and US Open finals, losing to Elena Rybakina and Iga Swiatek respectively.
- **2023**: Reached Wimbledon final for the second consecutive year, losing to Markéta Vondroušová.
- **2021**: Won Birmingham Classic, her first WTA title.
- **Multiple WTA 1000 titles**: Madrid (2022), etc.
- **Olympic Participant**: Represented Tunisia at Tokyo 2020 Olympics.
- **First Arab Woman**: To reach a Grand Slam final and break into the top 10.

## Rivalries
- **Jabeur vs. Swiatek**: Competitive matches, often with Swiatek having the edge.
- **Jabeur vs. Rybakina**: Their 2022 Wimbledon final was a classic, with Rybakina prevailing.
- **Jabeur vs. Sabalenka**: Several tight matches, often going to three sets.
- **Jabeur vs. Gauff**: Contrasting styles, with Jabeur's variety vs. Gauff's athleticism.

## Legacy (Early Career)
- **African Tennis Pioneer**: First African woman to reach a Grand Slam final and break into the top 10.
- **Tunisian Tennis Icon**: Tunisia's most successful tennis player in history.
- **Shot Maker Extraordinaire**: Demonstrated the effectiveness of variety and creativity in the modern power game.
- **Next Generation Leader**: Part of the group of players shaping the future of women's tennis with her unique style.

## Fun Facts & Trivia
- Comes from a sporting family; her father Ridha Jabeur was a volleyball player, and her mother Samira Elloumi was a volleyball player.
- Known for her trademark headbands and colorful on-court attire.
- Avid football fan and supporter of Esperance Sportive de Tunis.
- Fluent in Arabic, French, and English.
- Known for her charitable work, particularly supporting sports programs for girls in Tunisia.
