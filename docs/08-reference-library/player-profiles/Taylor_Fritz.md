# Taylor Fritz

## Overview
- **Full Name**: Taylor Harry Fritz
- **Date of Birth**: October 28, 1997
- **Nationality**: American
- **Turned Pro**: 2015
- **Plays**: Right-handed (two-handed backhand)
- **Highest Ranking**: No. 5 (achieved September 2022)
- **Career Titles**: 8 ATP singles titles
- **Grand Slam Titles**: 0 (best: quarterfinalist Wimbledon 2022, US Open 2022)
- **Career Prize Money**: Over $15 million

## Playing Style & Strengths
**Powerful Serve**: Fritz possesses one of the best serves in American tennis, capable of generating tremendous pace and accuracy.

**Forehand Weapon**: His forehand is a major asset, capable of generating immense pace and topspin, allowing him to dictate play from the baseline.

**Two-Handed Backhand**: Solid and reliable, capable of handling high-bouncing balls and generating pace when needed.

**All-Court Game**: Comfortable playing from the baseline, approaching the net, and defending with his athletic ability.

**Mental Toughness**: Has shown resilience in big matches, including coming back from deficits.

**Weaknesses**: Can sometimes struggle with consistency, particularly when his timing is off. Movement, while good, is not elite compared to the very best. Return of serve can be a liability at times.

- **2018**: Won ATP 250 title in Eastbourne.
