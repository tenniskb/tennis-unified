# Holger Rune

## Overview
- **Full Name**: Holger Vitus Nødskov Rune
- **Date of Birth**: April 29, 2003
- **Nationality**: Danish
- **Turned Pro**: 2020
- **Plays**: Right-handed (two-handed backhand)
- **Highest Ranking**: No. 4 (achieved August 2023)
- **Career Titles**: 6 ATP singles titles
- **Grand Slam Titles**: 0 (best: quarterfinalist 2022 French Open, 2023 Wimbledon, 2024 Australian Open)
- **Career Prize Money**: Over $10 million

## Playing Style & Strengths
**Aggressive Baseline Game**: Rune plays with tremendous intensity and aggression, often looking to dictate play from the first shot.

**Powerful Forehand**: His forehand is a major weapon, capable of generating tremendous pace and topspin.

**Two-Handed Backhand**: Solid and reliable, capable of handling high-bouncing balls and generating pace when needed.

**Fighting Spirit**: Known for his never-give-up attitude and intense competitiveness, often thriving in high-pressure situations.

**Athleticism**: Excellent movement and court coverage for his size, allowing him to retrieve difficult balls and stay in points.

**Weaknesses**: Can sometimes be overly aggressive, leading to unforced errors. Still developing consistency and point construction against top-tier opponents.

## Career Highlights
- **2022**: Won Paris Masters, defeating Novak Djokovic in the final (his first Masters 1000 title).
- **2022**: Reached French Open quarterfinals, his best Grand Slam result to date.
- **2023**: Won Munich Open and Geneva Open.
- **2023**: Reached Wimbledon quarterfinals.
- **2024**: Reached Australian Open quarterfinals.
- **2022**: Represented Denmark at the ATP Cup.
- **2021**: Won junior French Open boys' singles title.
