# Paula Badosa

## Overview
- **Full Name**: Paula Badosa Gibert
- **Date of Birth**: November 15, 1997
- **Nationality**: Spanish
- **Turned Pro**: 2015
- **Plays**: Right-handed (two-handed backhand)
- **Highest Ranking**: No. 2 (achieved April 2022)
- **Career Titles**: 4 WTA singles titles
- **Grand Slam Titles**: 0 (best: semifinalist French Open 2021, Wimbledon 2022)
- **Career Prize Money**: Over $10 million

## Playing Style & Strengths
**Aggressive Baseline Game**: Badosa combines powerful groundstrokes with excellent court coverage, allowing her to dictate play from the baseline.

**Powerful Forehand**: Her forehand is a major weapon, capable of generating tremendous pace and topspin.

**Two-Handed Backhand**: Solid and reliable, capable of handling pace and generating winners when needed.

**Varied Serve**: While not the biggest server, she can place her serve effectively and mix in spin to keep opponents off-balance.

**Mental Toughness**: Shown resilience in big matches, including coming back from deficits.

**Weaknesses**: Can sometimes be inconsistent with her timing; movement, while good, is not elite compared to the very best; second serve can be vulnerable at times.

## Career Highlights
- **2021**: Reached French Open semifinals, her best Grand Slam result at the time.
- **2022**: Reached Wimbledon semifinals.
- **2022**: Won Indian Wells WTA 1000 title, defeating Iga Swiatek in the final.
- **2021**: Won Belgrade WTA 250 title.
- **2020**: Won Palermo WTA 250 title.
- **Olympic Participant**: Represented Spain at Tokyo 2020 Olympics.
- **WTA Finals Participant**: 2021.

## Rivalries
- **Badosa vs. Swiatek**: Several tight matches, often with Swiatek having the edge on clay.
- **Badosa vs. Sabalenka**: Competitive matches, often going to three sets.
- **Badosa vs. Gauff**: Power vs. athleticism matchups.
- **Badosa vs. Jabeur**: Contrasting styles, with Badosa's power vs. Jabeur's variety.

## Legacy (Early Career)
- **Spanish Tennis Hope**: Spain's most promising female tennis player in recent years.
- **Top 10 Breakthrough**: Reached a career-high ranking of World No. 2 in 2022.
- **Big Title Winner**: Won a WTA 1000 title at a relatively young age.
- **Next Generation Leader**: Part of the group of players shaping the future of women's tennis alongside Swiatek, Sabalenka, and Rybakina.
