# Coco Gauff

## Overview
- **Full Name**: Cori "Coco" Gauff
- **Date of Birth**: March 13, 2004
- **Nationality**: American
- **Turned Pro**: 2018
- **Plays**: Right-handed (two-handed backhand)
- **Highest Ranking**: No. 3 (achieved August 2023)
- **Career Titles**: 8 WTA singles titles
- **Grand Slam Titles**: 1 (2023 US Open)
- **Career Prize Money**: Over $15 million

## Playing Style & Strengths
**Explosive Athleticism**: Gauff possesses exceptional speed, agility, and court coverage, allowing her to retrieve seemingly impossible shots.

**Powerful Serve**: Her serve has improved dramatically, capable of generating pace and accuracy, particularly effective on faster surfaces.

**Forehand Weapon**: Her forehand is a major asset, capable of generating tremendous pace and topspin, allowing her to dictate play from the baseline.

**Two-Handed Backhand**: Solid and reliable, capable of handling high-bouncing balls and generating pace when needed.

**Mental Toughness**: Shown remarkable composure for her age, including coming back from deficits in big matches.

**Weaknesses**: Can sometimes be inconsistent with her return of serve; still developing point construction against top-tier defenders; second serve can be vulnerable.

## Career Highlights
- **2019**: Reached Wimbledon fourth round at age 15, becoming the youngest player to qualify for Wimbledon in the Open Era.
- **2023**: Won US Open, defeating Aryna Sabalenka in the final.
- **2022**: Won French Open doubles (with Jessica Pegula).
- **Multiple WTA 1000 titles**: Washington (2023), etc.
- **Olympic Participant**: Represented USA at Tokyo 2020 Olympics.
- **WTA Finals**: Qualified multiple times.

## Rivalries
- **Gauff vs. Sabalenka**: Several tight matches, including the 2023 US Open semifinal.
- **Gauff vs. Swiatek**: Competitive matches, with Swiatek generally having the edge on clay.
- **Gauff vs. Pegula**: American rivalry with several entertaining matches.
- **Gauff vs. Rybakina**: Several three-set battles.

## Legacy (Early Career)
- **American Tennis Hope**: Considered one of the top American female tennis players of the current generation.
- **Teenage Slam Champion**: Won her first major at 19, joining an elite list of teenage Slam winners.
- **All-Court Player**: Demonstrated effectiveness of an athletic, all-court game in the modern era.
- **Next Generation Leader**: Part of the group of players shaping the future of women's tennis alongside Swiatek, Sabalenka, and Rybakina.

## Fun Facts & Trivia
- Comes from an athletic family; her father Corey Gauff played basketball at Georgia State University, and her mother Candi Gauff was a gymnast.
- Known for her signature headbands and colorful on-court attire.
- Avid reader and often shares book recommendations on social media.
- Fluent in English and some French.
- Known for her charitable work, particularly supporting children's causes in the United States.
