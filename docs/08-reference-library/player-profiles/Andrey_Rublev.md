# Andrey Rublev

## Overview
- **Full Name**: Andrey Andreyevich Rublev
- **Date of Birth**: October 20, 1997
- **Nationality**: Russian
- **Turned Pro**: 2014
- **Plays**: Right-handed (two-handed backhand)
- **Highest Ranking**: No. 5 (achieved September 2021)
- **Career Titles**: 15 ATP singles titles
- **Grand Slam Titles**: 0 (best: quarterfinalist Australian Open 2020, 2021, Wimbledon 2023, US Open 2020, 2022)
- **Career Prize Money**: Over $20 million

## Playing Style & Strengths
**Aggressive Baseline Game**: Rublev is known for his incredibly heavy and flat groundstrokes, particularly his forehand, which generates tremendous pace.

**Powerful Forehand**: His forehand is one of the heaviest on tour, often hitting winners from difficult positions.

**Two-Handed Backhand**: Solid and reliable, capable of handling high-bouncing balls and generating pace when needed.

**Fighting Spirit**: Known for his intense competitiveness and never-say-die attitude, often fighting back from difficult situations.

**Athleticism**: Good movement and court coverage, allowing him to extend points and wear down opponents.

**Weaknesses**: Can sometimes be inconsistent when his timing is off; movement, while good, is not elite compared to the very best; second serve can be vulnerable at times.

## Career Highlights
- **2020**: Reached US Open quarterfinals, his best Grand Slam result at the time.
- **2021**: Reached Australian Open quarterfinals.
- **2021**: Won ATP 500 titles in Hamburg and St. Petersburg.
- **2022**: Won ATP 500 titles in Dubai and Belgrade.
- **2023**: Reached Wimbledon quarterfinals.
- **Multiple ATP 250 titles**: Moscow (2020), Marseille (2021), Doha (2022), etc.
- **Olympic Participant**: Represented ROC at Tokyo 2020 Olympics.

## Legacy (Early Career)
- **Russian Tennis Star**: Russia's most consistent male tennis player in the late 2010s and early 2020s.
- **Ball-Striking Excellence**: Known for having one of the purest and most powerful ball-striking games on tour.
- **Consistent Performer**: Regularly reaches quarterfinals and semifinals at ATP Tour events.
- **Next Generation Leader**: Part of the group of players pushing the boundaries of men's tennis alongside Alcaraz, Sinner, and Rune.
