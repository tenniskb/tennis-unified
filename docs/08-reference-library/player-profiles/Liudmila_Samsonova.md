# Liudmila Samsonova

## Overview
- **Full Name**: Liudmila Samsonova
- **Date of Birth**: November 11, 1998
- **Nationality**: Russian
- **Turned Pro**: 2015
- **Plays**: Right-handed (two-handed backhand)
- **Highest Ranking**: No. 21 (achieved September 2022)
- **Career Titles**: 3 WTA singles titles
- **Grand Slam Titles**: 0 (best: quarterfinalist US Open 2021, Wimbledon 2022)
- **Career Prize Money**: Over $5 million

## Playing Style & Strengths
**Powerful Serve**: Samsonova possesses a strong serve capable of generating pace and accuracy, particularly effective on faster surfaces.

**Forehand Weapon**: Her forehand is struck with tremendous pace and flat trajectory, allowing her to dominate from the baseline.

**Two-Handed Backhand**: Solid and reliable, capable of handling pace and generating winners when needed.

**All-Court Game**: Comfortable playing from the baseline, approaching the net, and defending with her athletic ability.

**Mental Toughness**: Shown resilience in big matches, including coming back from deficits.

**Weaknesses**: Can sometimes be inconsistent with her timing; movement, while good, is not elite compared to the very best; return of serve can be a liability at times.

## Career Highlights
- **2021**: Reached US Open quarterfinals, her best Grand Slam result at the time.
- **2022**: Reached Wimbledon quarterfinals.
- **2021**: Won her first WTA title in Cleveland.
- **2022**: Won WTA 250 title in Hamburg.
- **2020**: Won WTA 250 title in Palermo.
- **Olympic Participant**: Represented ROC at Tokyo 2020 Olympics.
