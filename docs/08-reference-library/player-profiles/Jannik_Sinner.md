# Jannik Sinner

## Overview
- **Full Name**: Jannik Sinner
- **Date of Birth**: August 16, 2001
- **Nationality**: Italian
- **Turned Pro**: 2018
- **Plays**: Right-handed (two-handed backhand)
- **Highest Ranking**: No. 1 (achieved September 2024)
- **Career Titles**: 15 ATP singles titles
- **Grand Slam Titles**: 2 (2024 Australian Open, 2024 US Open)
- **Career Prize Money**: Over $20 million

## Playing Style & Strengths
**Complete All-Court Game**: Sinner possesses one of the most complete games on tour, with exceptional groundstrokes, movement, and tactical intelligence.

**Exceptional Ball Striking**: Known for his incredibly clean, flat striking ability on both forehand and backhand, generating tremendous pace with minimal apparent effort.

**Movement and Court Coverage**: Elite footwork and recovery ability allow him to turn defense into offense instantly.

**Mental Composure**: Remarkable calmness and focus under pressure, rarely showing emotion during matches.

**Weaknesses**: Can sometimes be too passive against extreme power players, and his serve, while solid, is not yet a dominant weapon compared to the very best.

## Career Highlights
- **2021**: First ATP title at Sofia Open
- **2022**: Reached first Grand Slam quarterfinal at French Open
- **2023**: Won Masters 1000 titles in Toronto and Cincinnati
- **2024**: Won first two Grand Slam titles (Australian Open and US Open)
- **2024**: Achieved World No. 1 ranking for the first time
- **Multiple ATP 500 titles**: Beijing, Vienna, Rotterdam
- **2023 ATP Finals Finalist**: Lost to Novak Djokovic in the final

## Rivalries
- **Sinner vs. Alcaraz**: Emerging rivalry with frequent matches, often going to three sets; represents the next generation of tennis.
- **Sinner vs. Djokovic**: Competitive matches, with Sinner securing notable wins over the veteran.
- **Sinner vs. Medvedev**: Several tight matches, with Sinner gaining the upper hand in recent encounters.

## Legacy (Early Career)
- **Next Generation Leader**: Considered one of the leaders of tennis' next generation alongside Carlos Alcaraz.
- **Italian Tennis Icon**: Already Italy's most successful male tennis player in the Open Era.
- **Complete Player Archetype**: Representing the modern ideal of a tennis player with no glaring weaknesses.

## Fun Facts & Trivia
- Former competitive skier before focusing on tennis full-time at age 13.
- Known for his meticulous preparation and routine-oriented approach.
- Fluent in Italian, German, and English.
- Works with renowned coach Riccardo Piatti and fitness advisor Marco Panichi.
