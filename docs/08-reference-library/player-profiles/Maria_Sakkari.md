# Maria Sakkari

## Overview
- **Full Name**: Maria Sakkari
- **Date of Birth**: July 25, 1995
- **Nationality**: Greek
- **Turned Pro**: 2015
- **Plays**: Right-handed (two-handed backhand)
- **Highest Ranking**: No. 3 (achieved March 2022)
- **Career Titles**: 6 WTA singles titles
- **Grand Slam Titles**: 0 (best: finalist 2021 US Open, semifinalist 2021 French Open, 2022 Wimbledon)
- **Career Prize Money**: Over $10 million

## Playing Style & Strengths
**Powerful Baseline Game**: Sakkari is known for her explosive groundstrokes, particularly her forehand, which generates tremendous pace and topspin.

**Excellent Movement**: Her footwork and court coverage are among the best on tour, allowing her to retrieve difficult balls and turn defense into offense.

**Varied Serve**: While not the biggest server, she can place her serve effectively and mix in spin to keep opponents off-balance.

**Two-Handed Backhand**: Solid and reliable, capable of handling pace and generating winners when needed.

**Mental Toughness**: Shown resilience in big matches, including coming back from deficits.

**Weaknesses**: Can sometimes be inconsistent with her timing; second serve can be vulnerable at times; return of serve can be a liability against extreme spin.

## Career Highlights
- **2021**: Reached US Open final, losing to Emma Raducanu in straight sets.
- **2021**: Reached French Open semifinals, losing to Barbora Krejcikova in three sets.
- **2022**: Reached Wimbledon semifinals, losing to Ons Jabeur in three sets.
- **2020**: Won Istanbul Cup.
- **Multiple WTA 1000 titles**: Rome (2021), etc.
- **Olympic Participant**: Represented Greece at Tokyo 2020 Olympics.
- **WTA Finals Participant**: 2021.
