# The Serve - the stances

**The Serve: Stances**

**Dr. Brian Gordon**

Of all the technical issues in serving the choice of stance is one of
the mostly debated - often hotly. In this fifth segment in his amazing
series, Brian outlines his view that the platform stance is the most
effective and efficient way to generate ground forces that in turn set
up the various rotations he has outlined in previous articles.
