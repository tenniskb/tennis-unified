# The Next ATP Forehand

**The Next ATP Forehand?**

**Dr. Brian Gordon**

Tennis is in a state of perpetual evolution. Is that possible true of
the ATP forehand? In this article, Dr. Brian Gordon discusses the
differences in his original, minimalistic forehand model and the
forehands of players like Nick Krygios and Jack Sock.

He explains the differences in the backswings and what that means for
the forehand swing, and then shows how he feels new elements could be
synthesized with the original ATP fundamentals. Original work from the
leading researcher in the world in tennis bio-mechanics.
