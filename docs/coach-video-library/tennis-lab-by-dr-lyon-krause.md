# Tennis Lab by Dr. Lyon Krause

*Tennis Lab*

Tennis Lab, led by Dr. Lyon Krause—a PhD in Sports Science and one of the world’s foremost experts in tennis equipment—is operated in partnership with Tennis Australia. Known for working with top ATP and WTA players, including custom racket setups and elite stringing services at the Australian Open and other major pro tournaments, Tennis Lab is at the global forefront of racket and string technology.

Their facility is the only public-access Hawk-Eye-equipped court in the world, using pro-grade analytics to test equipment across speed, spin, depth, and accuracy. Every video in this series draws from that world-class data, providing players of all levels with insights that were once only available to the game’s elite. By watching these videos and implementing the advice, players can make smarter, science-backed decisions about their rackets, strings, and setups—leading to noticeable improvements in control, power, injury prevention, and overall confidence on court.

Whether you're a junior, a high-performance athlete, or a recreational player, the tools and knowledge from Tennis Lab will transform how you approach your gear. And soon, Coach Life members will gain exclusive access to Tennis Lab’s free Racket Recommendation Tool—giving every player a custom-fit equipment experience backed by real data. This 30-video series covers every major topic in tennis gear, from string tension, gauge, and material type to racket length, mass, and swing weight.

You’ll learn how pros use tiny changes in string setup to gain 5–10% performance boosts, how to customize rackets with lead tape, the real differences between soft and stiff frames, and how most players are using the wrong racket without realizing it. You’ll also discover why Luxilon ALU Power is so popular, how doubles specialists can benefit from shorter rackets, and why vibration dampeners are just a placebo. Each video is short, practical, and based on cutting-edge research and thousands of real-life tests with players from juniors to Grand Slam champions.

Whether you're looking to improve spin, gain more depth on your shots, protect against injury, or finally understand what string tension really does, this series gives you the clarity and tools to upgrade your game instantly. It’s the most comprehensive, data-driven equipment guide available anywhere—and it’s exclusive to Coach Life.
