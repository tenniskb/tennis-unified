# Michal Kaznowski

*An unsung hero and brilliant coach, Michal Kaznowski shaped one of the*

greatest tennis players of all time, Iga Swiatek, from the tender age of 10 to 16. He shares the intricate details of utilizing technical expertise combined with a relentless focus on speed, footwork, and strengthening exercises to forge Swiatek's explosive power. His psychological insights into player development rank among the most valuable advice in tennis.

Michal reveals how he incorporated a Theraband in daily training to enhance Swiatek's elbow positioning, cultivating the remarkable linear power evident in her forehand. Furthermore, his CoachLife series delves into 'active preparation,' spotlighting the small, precise steps that are now a hallmark of Swiatek's game, enabling her unmatched power generation. The series also explores 'zone training,' meticulously designed to heighten consistency and precision in her shots, offering viewers a comprehensive guide to replicating Swiatek's formidable playing style.
