# Todd Larkham

*With over 10,000 hours spent coaching Nick Kyrgios both on-court and*

through match observations, Todd Larkham has been instrumental in shaping Nick's tennis career. Todd shares profound insights into his belief that a combination of innate talent, exceptional coaching, and a conducive training environment is essential for cultivating champions. He reflects on Nick's fierce competitiveness and his strategic approach to training hours, which included a comprehensive focus on forehand development through extensive drills.

As a player, Todd participated in 18 Grand Slam main draws, achieving a career-high ranking of 136 in singles and competing against some of the world's top players. He recounts discovering a nine-year-old Nick at a local tournament in Canberra, immediately recognizing him as the most formidable competitor he had ever seen. Todd's contributions to CoachLife include detailed instructional content on forehand techniques and one of the most effective kick serve videos available.

His lessons are packed with essential tips and invaluable advice that aim to significantly enhance your game. Additionally, Todd discusses various drills for improving forehands and serves and shares his research on the diverse pathways athletes can take to break into the top 100. This wealth of knowledge not only underscores Todd’s extensive experience but also his dedication to developing the next generation of tennis talent
