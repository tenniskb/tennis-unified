# Adriano Fuorivia — Coach Video Masterclass

!!! info "Coach Profile & Overview"
    Adriano Fuorivia

Canadian Coaching Legend and Junior Coach to Denis Shapovalov
Adriano Fuorivia, a renowned Canadian coaching legend, worked with Denis
Shapovalov from the age of 12 through to 18. Under his guidance, Denis
transformed from a player with no national titles to a champion winning
national titles, junior Grand Slams, and reaching the semifinals of the
Masters Series in Canada, where he famously defeated Rafael Nadal.
Fuorivia’s coaching philosophy is centered around taking the ball early,
creating linear power, and using the legs to generate explosiveness. He
emphasizes the importance of a wide base, consistently developing and
stressing this fundamental aspect in his training. His unique and
fascinating content includes brilliant specific drills, such as using
targets in a distinctive way to track where the ball finishes, the
second serve plus one drill, turning defense into offense, and how he
future-proofed Denis’s game by developing a playing style that resembled
an adult, easing his transition to the professional circuit.
Adriano also shares invaluable insights into footwork and court
positioning, explaining how recovering to a base position further back
allows players to track the ball earlier and create more opportunities
to step in. His core beliefs focus on pushing players to their limits
and not allowing any excuses from players or parents. His famous mantra,
"I'm  not in the business of excuses," was pivotal in developing Denis’s
mental toughness, teaching him not to bail out of points or resort to
low-percentage tennis.
The content Adriano offers is a combination of drills, core philosophies
visible in Denis’s game, emphasizing legs, explosiveness, and attacking
any ball when inside the court. He shares fascinating stories of Denis’s
development, including training sessions at 1 AM and using an eye patch
to strengthen the weaker eye. His brilliant serving progression video is
a must-see for developing players.
Additionally, at the end of the content, there will be three podcast
videos available, where Adriano discusses his coaching experiences,
philosophies, and answers questions, providing further insights into his
successful coaching strategies.
Join Coachlife to delve into the exceptional coaching world of Adriano
Fuorivia and learn from the best!
