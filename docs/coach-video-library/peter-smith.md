# Peter Smith

*Arguably the greatest college tennis coach in history, Peter Smith*

boasts five NCAA championships with USC and has played a pivotal role in the development of two world number 1 doubles players. His coaching system notably contributed to the collegiate and subsequent professional success of top 20 ATP player Steve Johnson over four years. Peter’s coaching prowess extends further, having guided Glenn Weiner from the age of 12 and nurturing Iva Jovic from the same age to a grand slam junior champion and a pro tournament winner at just 15.

Peter's coaching philosophy emphasizes fostering an aggressive mindset, guiding players to overcome overthinking with concise, clear guidance that encourages self-discovery within defined boundaries, without making unnecessary technical adjustments. His content on CoachLife is lauded for providing some of the most effective volleying strategies, enriched with insights and contributions from Steve Johnson and Iva Jovic. Additionally, his platform features brilliant drills specifically designed to enhance your doubles game and backhand, making his CoachLife offerings an invaluable resource for players seeking to elevate their game to the highest levels In addition to Peter's insightful instructional videos, he also offers personalized video analysis of your strokes and the opportunity to engage in an absolutely invaluable 30-minute or 1-hour Zoom consultation.

During these sessions, you can delve deep into the world of college tennis with Peter. Explore topics such as recruitment strategies, understanding what coaches are looking for, choosing the right coach and program, and much more. These consultations are perfect for anyone looking to maximize their potential in college tennis and gain expert guidance tailored to their individual needs.
