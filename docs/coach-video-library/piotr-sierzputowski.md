# Piotr Sierzputowski

Piotr Sierzputowski is a world-class Polish tennis coach who developed Iga Świątek from her junior career through her historic 2020 Roland Garros Grand Slam victory, earning him the 2020 WTA Coach of the Year award.

His coaching focuses on explosive movement, heavy baseline groundstrokes, and mental composure under high-pressure competitive environments.
