# Jeff Drock — Coach Video Masterclass

!!! info "Coach Profile & Overview"
    Jeff Drock

Jeff Drock, Coco Gauff’s junior fitness coach, is credited with building
the foundation for her exceptional movement and athleticism on the
court. With a master’s degree in exercise science and over 20 years of
experience, Jeff has worked with numerous pro players and top juniors
worldwide, establishing himself as a leading figure in tennis-specific
speed, footwork, and conditioning.
Jeff’s training emphasizes balanced strength and functional movement
tailored to tennis, with a focus on agility, positioning, and injury
prevention. His program includes tennis-specific drills that teach
players to efficiently approach and recover from wide and close balls,
with exercises covering foot positioning, quick adjustments, and both
offensive and defensive control. He ensures balanced muscle development
to avoid common overuse injuries, addressing every muscle group from
hamstrings to hip flexors, wrists, and forearms.
Jeff believes in starting training early, using bodyweight exercises,
medicine balls, and resistance bands to build resilience without heavy
weights. His approach also instills focus, resilience, and determination
in young players—key traits for those aiming to compete at high levels.
His Coachlife program covers essential skills like split-step patterns,
reaction drills, and advanced match-like scenarios to build footwork,
positioning, and recovery. Through his expertise, players gain the
physical and mental edge needed for elite performance.
