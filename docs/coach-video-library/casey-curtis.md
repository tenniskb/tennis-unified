# Casey Curtis

*Welcome to Casey Curtis's Coaching Series on Coachlife*

Renowned for his expertise in developing one of the biggest serves in tennis history, Casey Curtis is a master coach who brings decades of experience to Coachlife. For over 10 years, Casey coached Milos Raonic from his early days as a beginner, working intensively with him for six hours a day to shape his game, most notably developing Raonic's serve into one of the most formidable weapons in tennis history. His specialized approach to teaching the serve has helped numerous ATP pros climb from outside the Top 300 to the Top 30 after just short periods of focused serve training.

In this series, Casey unveils his proven techniques in the Serve Masterclass, where he focuses on maximizing power, maintaining rhythm, and perfecting the ball toss, all while avoiding common mistakes that can undermine your serve. You'll also get a sneak peek into his methods for building mental toughness, fostering a winning mindset, and understanding the evolving demands of modern tennis. Additionally, Casey shares behind-the-scenes stories from his 9-year journey with Milos Raonic, including insights on how a hands-off approach from Milos's parents and dedicated practice shaped him into a top tennis professional.

You’ll learn the importance of balancing skill development with tournament play, managing pressure effectively, and setting realistic yet challenging goals to elevate your game to the next level. Whether you're looking to refine your serve, strengthen your mental game, or understand the nuances of modern tennis strategy, Casey Curtis’s content on Coachlife is designed to provide you with the tools and knowledge to excel.
